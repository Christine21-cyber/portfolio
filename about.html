ABOUT ME

Why I chose Computer Science — and where I'm headed

Why Computer Science

I went into tech because it's one of the few places left where you can build an entire world out of nothing but an idea and a keyboard. What keeps me here is that specific "aha" moment — the hours spent staring at a broken piece of code, and then the second the logic clicks, the error disappears, and it just works. Frustration turning into mastery in a single keystroke never gets old.

That mix of problem-solving and creation is what pulled me toward software engineering specifically. I like that a good solution is both logical and, quietly, a creative one.

Career goals

My goal is to grow into a well-rounded software engineer — someone equally comfortable building for mobile as for the web, who can take a project from an idea through to something people can actually use. In the nearer term, I'm working toward an internship where I can apply what I'm learning in a real team environment.
